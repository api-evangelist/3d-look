FitXpress turns two smartphone photos and a short profile into structured body data: 80+ body measurements, body composition estimates, a 3D model, and Body Progress comparisons between scans. Enterprise customers integrate FitXpress via an Application Programming Interface (API) or Software Development Kit (SDK) to support telehealth, weight management, wellness, connected fitness, insurance, and research workflows. This FAQ provides a general overview of the data, privacy, security, and regulatory aspects.

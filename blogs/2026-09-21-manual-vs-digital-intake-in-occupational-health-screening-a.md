Where a screening process requires both a health questionnaire and body measurements, those tasks often take place during the appointment. The candidate or employee completes the form, the clinic staff takes measurements with a tape measure, and the information is entered into the record before clinical review begins. Moving eligible steps online changes when the record becomes available and how much work remains inside the appointment.
